01 / Selected work
FEATURED CAPSTONE

SecureGate AI

Making security findings actionable before deployment.

Explore repository

A pipeline can pass and still carry security risks. With SecureGate AI, I brought scan results, deployment decisions and remediation guidance into one DevSecOps platform, connecting technical findings to the decision to ship.

The platform receives reports from CI/CD runners, normalizes findings and evaluates security gates. Teams can review risk scores, reports, notifications and trends, with local AI assistance to understand what needs fixing.

02Normalize & scoreFindings into risk signals
